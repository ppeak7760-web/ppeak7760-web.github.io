Privacy Policy

Tacs X Poster · last updated 15 October 2026

Tacs X Poster is a private tool run by Tacs.X. It is used only by us, with our own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

When we sign in with Google, the tool asks for permission to upload videos to our YouTube channel and to read our channel's videos and view counts (YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).

How the data is used

Only to upload our own short videos, with their titles, descriptions and tags, and to see how they perform. We do not collect data about other people, we do not sell or share any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

The sign-in token and view statistics are stored only on our own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's.

YouTube and Google

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which we delete the stored token.
